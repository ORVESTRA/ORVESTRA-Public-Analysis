# ORVESTRA™ Public Analysis

**Business Analysis for Real Decisions.**

Business Models • Value Capture • Productization • Growth Systems

ORVESTRA™ Public Analysis examines observable developments in businesses, industries, and markets through evidence-based analysis.

The objective is simple:

**Identify what is changing, distinguish what the evidence establishes, and examine what the change may mean for business structure and real-world decisions.**

## Analysis Areas

### Business Models

How businesses create, deliver, and capture value as markets, technologies, regulation, costs, and customer behavior change.

### Value Capture

Where economic value is created, where it moves, who captures it, and how structural changes can alter that distribution.

### Productization

How capabilities, services, technologies, and knowledge are transformed into repeatable and commercially usable offerings.

### Growth Systems

How businesses expand through markets, capacity, distribution, partnerships, infrastructure, and other growth structures.

## Public Analysis

Public analysis focuses on developments that can be examined using traceable public evidence.

Each publication is designed to separate:

**Observed Development → Evidence → Structural Meaning → Business Implication → Limits**

Public analysis does not disclose internal analytical methods, proprietary research processes, or non-public working materials.
