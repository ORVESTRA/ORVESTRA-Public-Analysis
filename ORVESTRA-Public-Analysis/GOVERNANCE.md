## Purpose

This repository contains public business analysis produced by ORVESTRA Business Architecture Lab.

Public analysis examines observable developments across:

- Business Models
- Value Capture
- Productization
- Growth Systems

The purpose is to provide evidence based analysis that improves understanding of business structures, commercial change, and their implications for real decisions.

---

## Evidence Standard

Material claims should be supported by identifiable evidence where possible.

ORVESTRA distinguishes between:

1. Documented fact
2. Publicly reported information
3. Structural observation
4. Analytical interpretation
5. Information that is not publicly established

Information that is unavailable or unsupported should not be presented as known fact.

Analytical conclusions should remain proportional to the available evidence.

---

## Public Analysis Boundary

Public analysis may explain:

- What has been observed
- What available evidence establishes
- Relevant structural distinctions
- Business significance
- Observable implications
- Evidence limitations
- Developments that warrant continued observation

Public publication does not disclose:

- Proprietary analytical methods
- Internal research workflows
- Scoring or evidence weighting systems
- Internal dependency mapping
- Non-public working material
- Private engagements
- Restricted information
- Internal DGCP™ operational architecture

Public analysis demonstrates analytical capability through the published work itself.

It does not disclose the internal process used to produce that capability.

---

## Independence

ORVESTRA independently selects the companies, industries, markets, and commercial developments examined in this repository.

Publication does not imply sponsorship, partnership, endorsement, engagement, authorization, or any commercial relationship with an organization referenced.

---

## Analytical Boundaries

ORVESTRA public analysis does not constitute investment, securities, legal, accounting, tax, or financial product advice.

Analysis of a company does not constitute a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold its securities.

Readers remain responsible for their own decisions.

---

## Corrections and Record Integrity

Material factual errors should be corrected when identified.

Corrections should preserve transparency and record integrity where practical.

Analytical disagreement alone does not constitute a factual error.

---

## Framework Governance

This repository operates under the DGCP™ framework and the broader ORVESTRA governance structure.

DGCP™ provides the governance context for evidence discipline, traceability, observation, verification, analytical boundaries, record integrity, and continuous proof.
